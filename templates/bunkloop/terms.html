Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 29 Aug 2026. By using Bunkloop you agree to these terms.

1. Campus Marketplace

Bunkloop is a university-scoped marketplace for students to list, browse, buy or rent items within their own university. Listings are visible only to students of the same university.

2. Eligibility & Verification

  • Only students with a valid university email domain (as configured for each University, e.g. thapar.edu) may register.
  • OTP verification via sendotp.email is required before an account is created. The verification state is stored server-side (session/cache) and the OTP code itself is never stored by Bunkloop.
  • Identity photo must be a live camera capture, not a gallery upload, and is used only for trust & safety.

3. Listings & Transactions

  • Sellers may list items with title, category, price, listing type (selling/renting), condition and up to 4 live photos.
  • Buyers and sellers communicate via the in-app messaging system. Conversations are tied to a listing and isolated by university.
  • Checkout creates an Order with status flow: pending → paid → confirmed → shipped → delivered → completed / cancelled. Payments are currently mocked; real Razorpay/Stripe integration will be added.
  • Sellers may remove their own listings via My items → Remove or Item detail → Remove Listing unless protected orders exist.

4. Messaging

Messages are persisted in PostgreSQL (source of truth) and delivered in real-time via WebSocket/Redis. Rate limits (default 30/min) and validation (max 5000 chars) apply. University isolation is enforced — you can only message sellers from your university.

5. Privacy & Data

  • We store only necessary profile data (name, university, email, contact, etc.) and never expose your otp_email_key or OTP codes to the frontend, logs, or Git.
  • Media is stored under /media/ (or S3 in production). Cookies are used for session, CSRF, and consent.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and a consent banner on the home screen. You may accept or ask me later; the banner will reappear on next home visit until you accept. See our cookie banner for choices.

7. Acceptable Use

Do not list prohibited, illegal, or misleading items. Do not spam messaging. We may moderate or remove listings and suspend accounts for violations.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. For questions contact support via the in-app contact seller flow.
